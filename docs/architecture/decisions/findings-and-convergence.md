# Findings and convergence — decisions

Decision log for [Findings and convergence](../findings-and-convergence.md). Entries are append-only: a
replaced entry gets a `Superseded by #M: <clause>.` note appended and is
never rewritten or deleted. The detail doc describes the current design.

- Reviewer prompts limit blocking findings to stated or signed requirements, regressions in touched behaviour and introduced security or data-loss risk, and future follow-ups are accepted on blocking reviews but never become coder or planner work (#1389).
- Human-only exact-head evidence is a separate channel from findings: an obligation owned by the requesting reviewer, answered through a freeze boundary at a clean head (#1068).
- Risk-matrix rows whose evidence cannot be tool-captured convert into the same obligation kind, automatically after N capture-limited attempts or on operator request, with signed-evidence references minted only on owner clearance at the frozen head (#1325).
- Conjunctive findings carry ordered sub-items whose status is reviewer-authoritative, while coder `addressed_sub_items` stay advisory claims (#958).
- Malformed sub-items in a reviewer response degrade to a plain finding with a recorded degradation instead of rejecting the envelope (#924).
- The PR fix loop reuses the plan step-back classification, clustering findings by mapped location across heads, so repeated new findings near one span trigger one generalizing coder turn and a reviewer sweep (#1251).
- A separate non-convergence stop catches items that persist across K+1 reviews regardless of file, because the clustered trigger only counts new findings near one span (#1372).
- The evidence-only stall stop ends a run whose only blocking items are tagged with unsatisfied risk-matrix rows for K rounds, derived from persisted evidence so a resume recomputes the same decision (#1324).
- The unchanged-head stop grants one extra review round per head when the follow-up superseded a parent-observed failure with a receipt from its own frozen acquisition catalog that a surviving citation also cites; uncited supersession and absence from the bounded projection never count, so the exception stays bounded and tool-decided (#1626, 2026-10-24).
- The PR step-back coder turn may answer `Approach alternative:` instead of generalizing; that head-bound answer stops for a human decision after the managed-CI continuity grant and again at round start on resume, because redesigning beyond the approved plan is a human scope decision (#1628, 2026-10-24).
- An admitted evidence-conversion record bounds same-round peer visibility, resume and partial-round recovery, so a same-head conversion repeat no longer trips the partial-publish guard on its own pre-conversion pass, while repeat-pass publications stay peers; the round is not advanced, because round budgets and resume would change for no extra isolation (#1664, 2026-10-25).
- A full-board pass that overrides a staged scheduler decision rewrites the decision to the board that runs, and a recovery whose requested conversion boundary cannot be located advises no deletion, so a tool-created conversion repeat neither fails qualification on contradictory scheduler metadata nor lists its earlier pass for deletion (#1664, 2026-10-25).
- The unchanged-head evidence-progress grant reads earlier rounds only through the previous round's bounded evidence projection, so after a resume a parent-observed failure evicted from it cannot earn the grant; this stays as documented conservative behaviour rather than persisting more evidence (#1639, 2026-10-25).

- Tool-verified converted capture publications require semantic review after their ordered boundary, reconstructed from existing admitted history without a new persisted payload (#1734).

- Optional converted recovery capture requires a coder slot within the restored effective ceiling; at an exhausted ceiling unresolved rows instead reach the normal clean-head evidence freeze, preserving ordinary blocker stops and existing budgets without new state (#1730, 2026-10-27).

- Evidence-stall and evidence-freeze stops publish one bounded best-effort audit diagnostic per stopped invocation for history export; mandatory freeze publication remains authoritative and precedes the audit, whose failure preserves the identical exit-4 exception (#1685, 2026-10-27).

- Accepted citations from the frozen acquisition reserve bounded projection capacity before history; protected overflow stops the handoff explicitly, prior legacy declarations are replaced, and newly verified canonical receipt-only rows may use the existing single per-head progress grant without changing reviewer or CI authority (#1758, 2026-10-28).

- 2026-10-30: Failed PR acknowledgement repair summaries explicitly mark an open-round ledger boundary, preserving blocking obligations across same-head restart without counting the summary as reconciliation (#1791).

- 2026-10-30: PR-record/branch observation uses three bounded samples before head-bound lifecycle publication and unchanged accounting; persistent strict descendant lag stops without granting branch-tip authority. Operator rewind/re-push recovery stays manual, and existing origin-specific cleanup and exact-head CI/merge gates remain in force (#1801).

- 2026-11-06: Signed-source routing is owned by `requirement_evidence.py`. Conflicting classifications stop conservatively. - 2026-11-19: Option A for coder-disposition scope: signed sources the shared projection routes out of coder scope (evidence-only, human-only) are reviewer-owned and never coder-dispositioned. Coder prompts list them read-only, reviewer guidance names them as needing no coder disposition, and validators report a known reviewer-owned ID as out of scope instead of unknown. The set derives from the same projection on live and resumed rounds. Option B (coder-visible no-op dispositions) was rejected because it would add coder work that cannot change the evidence outcome. Mechanically rewriting reviewer findings that demand such dispositions remains optional follow-up hardening (#2020).

- 2026-11-19: A carried coder dispute is released when the existing effective signed-requirement set has a requirement created strictly after the dispute time, taken from the newest admitted coder follow-up record; an unknown time (including this invocation's not-yet-observed re-dispute), a tie or an unparseable timestamp holds. Per-comment decision association, item-ID matching against decision text and REST content binding were rejected as out of scope; any newer requirement releases every older dispute because release only re-routes work and a re-dispute re-arms the stop. The stop notice lists only IDs, times and recovery steps and is posted best-effort. Requirement-text placement and parser admission of tool-published text stay with #2028 (#2025).

- 2026-11-19: Within one invocation, a round that follows a carried dispute refetches the admitted PR context at the round boundary (as converted capture already does), so a signed answer posted between consecutive rounds releases the dispute without a rerun; other rounds keep reusing the post-coder snapshot to avoid an extra read (#2025).

- 2026-11-19: The final evidence gate also accepts a requester's same-head resolution from the approvals the round's scheduler counted as qualifying (unchanged-head carry or selective pause), not only current-round records. Carried records are re-admitted by identity against the gate's authenticated read; the requester's newest complete review at the head governs. The freeze baseline and frozen-resume new-signed-ID check are unchanged; a PR already frozen by the earlier behavior still resumes with a fresh signed comment (#2019).

- 2026-11-22: Every human-decision stop raises `HumanDecisionRequiredError` (exit 4) with one three-part message: what happened, `To clear:` naming one action, and `Safe to automate:` with a reason, and nothing after it. Signed-record and board stops that were plain `AgentLoopError` (exit 1) were converted. The plan step-back and primary-stall stops use a dual-inheritance pre-panel subclass, so pre-panel handlers still match. Errors are built only by the factory or from registered renderers, and cross-function stops carry the error object. A static guard test enforces the rule. Exit-code remapping by message sniffing was rejected because it bypasses the in-loop human-decision handlers (#2069).

- 2026-11-22: Handlers that re-wrap a caught `AgentLoopError` around board, plan-lineage or plan-approval reads call `reraise_human_decision` first, so a human-decision stop raised during qualification or plan revalidation keeps exit 4 and its three-part shape. A static guard checks every try body that calls a function able to raise a stop, using a call graph over uniquely named module-level functions (#2069).

- 2026-11-22: Plan-approval classification fallbacks (input derivation, legacy plan history, signed supersession) re-raise human-decision stops instead of storing their text as underivable or undetermined reasons. A static guard flags such fallbacks through an import-resolved call graph. Agent-turn failure policies, the managed-CI ordinary-recovery fallback and the dry-run preview keep their degraded outcome; each is listed with a reason and tracked as follow-up (#2069).

- 2026-11-22: Historical PR board-binding validation and the managed-CI source-authority fallback also re-raise human-decision stops, so a misplaced signed amendment found during continuity checks no longer becomes a refused binding or an ordinary-recovery fallback. The fallback guard now covers every broad handler, named or not, so a handler that drops a stop for a degraded value is caught too (#2069).

- 2026-11-22: A reviewer approval missing the signed-requirements acknowledgement is no longer sent to a repair model; it directly upserts the dedicated acknowledgement obligation and the reviewer is asked again in the next round, because a second model must not author or reformat a reviewer's acknowledgement (#2066, #2091).

- 2026-11-22: One record-derived no-progress detector (`no_progress.py`, `--no-progress-rounds`, default 3, minimum 1) replaces the PR step-back sibling, non-convergence, approach-alternative and unchanged-head stops (stage 1 of #2067). It observes round N through the coder record numbered N+1 (settled ledger and dispatch head), so it works in the default sequential mode without a producer change; closure boundaries, missing records and dispatch-head mismatches break the window, and the live head must still match, so a moved head is reviewed first. The stop keeps each retired stop's routes and diagnostics as optional message sections and defers to the evidence stall when every unchanged item is evidence-owned. Step-back stays a prompt whose trigger and sweep are derived from review records only; legacy `step_back_entries` stay decodable, written for audit, and inert. The four PR threshold flags are accepted and ignored for one release. Disabling the stops by default and relying on `--max-rounds` was rejected because a stuck run would burn rounds with no item-level diagnostic; the per-head evidence-progress grant (#1626) was retired because a resolved item already restarts the window (#2097).

Inferring it from the bounded evidence was rejected because that evidence mixes in other turns' receipts and evicts rows past its cap (#2097).

- 2026-11-23: Exact-head human evidence becomes opt-in: `--pr-human-evidence freeze|advisory|off`, default `advisory` (stage 1 of #2063). One normalization choke point in `human_evidence_mode.py` removes native and converted human evidence obligations from non-freeze ledgers; `advisory` keeps them on a durable, non-blocking "Human checks requested" list stamped on orchestrator PR records and posted once per head at approval, and `off` drops them. Signed-source classification, the evidence stall and conversion run only in `freeze`, while the final head and signed-input reads and `_ensure_finalization_ready` still gate approval. With `--auto-merge` and a non-empty list the PR is left approved and unmerged and the run exits 0. A frozen PR resumed outside `freeze` is released with the new `advisory-mode` reason unless screenshot obligations stay frozen. Each list entry keeps its full removed item so a `freeze` resume restores typed provenance. Only un-gating the freeze was rejected because the requests would stay blocking ledger obligations held by partitions and dispatch, and classification would still stop runs before the gate. A new non-blocking ledger status was rejected because every partition and gate would have to learn it. Deleting the evidence modules is stage 2 (#2063).

- 2026-11-23: The PR reviewer signed-requirements acknowledgement is scoped to the shared `requirement_evidence.project` result under `--pr-human-evidence freeze`: reviewers emit the resolved marker when every non-reviewer-owned (not human-only or evidence-only) signed requirement is resolved, which is the set the acknowledgement creation and clearing checks already used. Advisory and off keep whole-set wording because classification is discarded there. The acknowledgement stop message no longer tells the operator to push a new head; it names current-head reviewer acknowledgement or coder dispositions. Dropping the marker check whenever any source is human-only was rejected because an unresolved code-role requirement could then pass approval, and a mode-blind prompt change would narrow attestation in advisory and off (#2083).

- 2026-11-23: Signed-source classification gains a `superseded` role for a source that a newer signed source from the same author replaces. Its fields are empty, and `validate_associations` checks the precondition (same author by `author_id` or login, strictly later parsed `created_at`; missing provenance fails closed). An agreed superseded source leaves the projection like evidence-only: reviewer-owned, with no coder work, acknowledgement or evidence. Prompts render superseded IDs as inert, apart from evidence-bearing reviewer-owned IDs. Re-asks to a reviewer that emitted no evidence requests no longer offer `human-only`. Relaxing the human-only linkage was rejected because an unlinked human-only source would silently leave the projection. A prompt-only rule to use evidence-only was rejected because it fails when the reviewer has no native item of its own to cite. Automatic orchestrator classification was rejected because it needs a new routing channel through projection, replay and round metadata (#2101).

- 2026-11-24: Plan reviews gain a third current-round list, `implementation_notes`, for in-scope findings that need no plan-design change and that the build or tests would surface anyway. Notes never block, are never carried items, planner input or filed issues, persist on the reviewer's plan round record, and are collected at approval from the settled records of the approved scope (newest authenticated boundary at or before the approving round), de-duplicated and bounded. They are listed in the approval comment (or a notes-only supplement when identical plan text is reapproved in a new scope) and reach the implementer and PR reviewers through equality-excluded `ApprovedPlanContext` fields, attached at every PR-facing recovery site under an inventory test; direct staged children inherit the parent's notes with an origin label. Changing only the reviewer prompt to omit such findings or move them to future follow-ups was rejected because the information would never reach the implementer or the PR reviewer. An approving-round-only fallback when the scope cannot be authenticated was rejected in favour of an explicit unavailable state that never blocks the run (#2145).
