# Findings and convergence — decisions

Decision log for [Findings and convergence](../findings-and-convergence.md). Entries are append-only: a
replaced entry gets a `Superseded by #M: <clause>.` note appended and is
never rewritten or deleted. The detail doc describes the current design.

- Reviewer prompts limit blocking findings to stated or signed requirements, regressions in touched behaviour and introduced security or data-loss risk, and future follow-ups are accepted on blocking reviews but never become coder or planner work (#1389).
- Human-only exact-head evidence is a separate channel from findings: an obligation owned by the requesting reviewer, answered through a freeze boundary at a clean head (#1068).
- Risk-matrix rows whose evidence cannot be tool-captured convert into the same obligation kind, automatically after N capture-limited attempts or on operator request, with signed-evidence references minted only on owner clearance at the frozen head (#1325).
- Conjunctive findings carry ordered sub-items whose status is reviewer-authoritative, while coder `addressed_sub_items` stay advisory claims (#958).
- Malformed sub-items in a reviewer response degrade to a plain finding with a recorded degradation instead of rejecting the envelope (#924).
- The PR fix loop reuses the plan step-back classification, clustering findings by mapped location across heads, so repeated new findings near one span trigger one generalizing coder turn and a reviewer sweep (#1251).
- A separate non-convergence stop catches items that persist across K+1 reviews regardless of file, because the clustered trigger only counts new findings near one span (#1372).
- The evidence-only stall stop ends a run whose only blocking items are tagged with unsatisfied risk-matrix rows for K rounds, derived from persisted evidence so a resume recomputes the same decision (#1324).
- The unchanged-head stop grants one extra review round per head when the follow-up superseded a parent-observed failure with a receipt from its own frozen acquisition catalog that a surviving citation also cites; uncited supersession and absence from the bounded projection never count, so the exception stays bounded and tool-decided (#1626, 2026-10-24).
- The PR step-back coder turn may answer `Approach alternative:` instead of generalizing; that head-bound answer stops for a human decision after the managed-CI continuity grant and again at round start on resume, because redesigning beyond the approved plan is a human scope decision (#1628, 2026-10-24).
- An admitted evidence-conversion record bounds same-round peer visibility, resume and partial-round recovery, so a same-head conversion repeat no longer trips the partial-publish guard on its own pre-conversion pass, while repeat-pass publications stay peers; the round is not advanced, because round budgets and resume would change for no extra isolation (#1664, 2026-10-25).
- A full-board pass that overrides a staged scheduler decision rewrites the decision to the board that runs, and a recovery whose requested conversion boundary cannot be located advises no deletion, so a tool-created conversion repeat neither fails qualification on contradictory scheduler metadata nor lists its earlier pass for deletion (#1664, 2026-10-25).
- The unchanged-head evidence-progress grant reads earlier rounds only through the previous round's bounded evidence projection, so after a resume a parent-observed failure evicted from it cannot earn the grant; this stays as documented conservative behaviour rather than persisting more evidence (#1639, 2026-10-25).

- Tool-verified converted capture publications require semantic review after their ordered boundary, reconstructed from existing admitted history without a new persisted payload (#1734).

- Optional converted recovery capture requires a coder slot within the restored effective ceiling; at an exhausted ceiling unresolved rows instead reach the normal clean-head evidence freeze, preserving ordinary blocker stops and existing budgets without new state (#1730, 2026-10-27).

- Evidence-stall and evidence-freeze stops publish one bounded best-effort audit diagnostic per stopped invocation for history export; mandatory freeze publication remains authoritative and precedes the audit, whose failure preserves the identical exit-4 exception (#1685, 2026-10-27).

- Accepted citations from the frozen acquisition reserve bounded projection capacity before history; protected overflow stops the handoff explicitly, prior legacy declarations are replaced, and newly verified canonical receipt-only rows may use the existing single per-head progress grant without changing reviewer or CI authority (#1758, 2026-10-28).

Operator rewind/re-push recovery stays manual, and existing origin-specific cleanup and exact-head CI/merge gates remain in force (#1801).

- 2026-11-19: A carried coder dispute is released when the existing effective signed-requirement set has a requirement created strictly after the dispute time, taken from the newest admitted coder follow-up record; an unknown time (including this invocation's not-yet-observed re-dispute), a tie or an unparseable timestamp holds. Per-comment decision association, item-ID matching against decision text and REST content binding were rejected as out of scope; any newer requirement releases every older dispute because release only re-routes work and a re-dispute re-arms the stop. The stop notice lists only IDs, times and recovery steps and is posted best-effort. Requirement-text placement and parser admission of tool-published text stay with #2028 (#2025).

- 2026-11-19: Within one invocation, a round that follows a carried dispute refetches the admitted PR context at the round boundary (as converted capture already does), so a signed answer posted between consecutive rounds releases the dispute without a rerun; other rounds keep reusing the post-coder snapshot to avoid an extra read (#2025).

- 2026-11-19: The final evidence gate also accepts a requester's same-head resolution from the approvals the round's scheduler counted as qualifying (unchanged-head carry or selective pause), not only current-round records. Carried records are re-admitted by identity against the gate's authenticated read; the requester's newest complete review at the head governs. The freeze baseline and frozen-resume new-signed-ID check are unchanged; a PR already frozen by the earlier behavior still resumes with a fresh signed comment (#2019).

- 2026-11-22: Every human-decision stop raises `HumanDecisionRequiredError` (exit 4) with one three-part message: what happened, `To clear:` naming one action, and `Safe to automate:` with a reason, and nothing after it. Signed-record and board stops that were plain `AgentLoopError` (exit 1) were converted. The plan step-back and primary-stall stops use a dual-inheritance pre-panel subclass, so pre-panel handlers still match. Errors are built only by the factory or from registered renderers, and cross-function stops carry the error object. A static guard test enforces the rule. Exit-code remapping by message sniffing was rejected because it bypasses the in-loop human-decision handlers (#2069).

- 2026-11-22: Handlers that re-wrap a caught `AgentLoopError` around board, plan-lineage or plan-approval reads call `reraise_human_decision` first, so a human-decision stop raised during qualification or plan revalidation keeps exit 4 and its three-part shape. A static guard checks every try body that calls a function able to raise a stop, using a call graph over uniquely named module-level functions (#2069).

- 2026-11-22: Historical PR board-binding validation and the managed-CI source-authority fallback also re-raise human-decision stops, so a misplaced signed amendment found during continuity checks no longer becomes a refused binding or an ordinary-recovery fallback.
